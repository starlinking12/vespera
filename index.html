/ 001   THE IDEA

Privacy should feel personal.

Vespera explores a different kind of messenger: one where a conversation starts with a choice, not a phone number. These are product goals, not shipping security guarantees.

FOUR PRODUCT CONCEPTS 01 — 04
01 / TEMPORAL CONTROL◌

Half-Life Decay

A configurable half-life for messages. The visual demo above uses exponential decay; deleting remote copies would require a real protocol and threat model.

02 / DEVICE PAIRING≋

Acoustic Bump

A proposed nearby-device pairing flow using sound in the 19–21 kHz range, without sharing a number. Hardware support and authentication need testing.

Kinetic Panic

A shake gesture designed to lock the app and request key erasure. A 5 ms purge is a target, not a verified hardware guarantee.
