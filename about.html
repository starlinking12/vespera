ABOUT / VESPERA

Privacy should feel natural.

Vespera is a design concept for conversations where identity starts with a handle and the important controls stay close. The security architecture is still a proposal, not a finished or audited app.

01 / WHY IT EXISTS

More space for the conversation. Less demanded up front.

A phone number can reveal more than someone wants to share. The proposed identity flow begins with a handle instead.

01 / IDENTITY

A handle to begin.

Find people through a name they choose. The account and recovery design still needs to be implemented and tested before privacy claims can be verified.

02 / KEYS

Understand who is on the other end.

The interface makes room for a contact key fingerprint and a verification step. Cryptographic behavior, key rotation, and recovery require independent review.

03 / DELIVERY

Move the message, not the history.

The proposed relay passes encrypted envelopes between clients. Server retention and real-world behavior are unverified until the implementation exists.

04 / LIFESPAN

Choose what remains.

Silent Lifespan and deletion options are shown in the local interface preview. They do not remove a message from another device or guarantee deletion.

02 / HOW WE THINK

Show the design. Test the promises.

The visual work is a starting point. Encryption, platform support, performance, and deletion guarantees belong in technical documentation and independent testing before anyone relies on them.
