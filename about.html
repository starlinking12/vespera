Vision & Philosophy

The architecture of sovereign conversation.

Communication does not require identification. For decades, consumer software has conflated connecting people with collecting their phone numbers and contact graphs.

Zero-Identifier Philosophy

Every mainstream communication tool begins with a compromise: submit a SIM card, associate a telephone number, and upload an address book. This architecture ties cryptographic identity to state-issued telecommunications credentials.

Vespera inverts this assumption. Conversations are ephemeral agreements between two independent devices. Your device creates private keys locally. No telephone numbers exist in the system, eliminating SIM-swap vulnerabilities and global directory indexing entirely.

Cryptographic Principles

The system is built upon four foundational security requirements designed from first principles:

1. Blind Relay Transmission

Relay nodes pass encrypted envelopes through transient queues without learning sender IP, recipient IP, or communication patterns.

2. Asynchronous Double Ratchet

Every message advances an ephemeral key ratchet. Compromising a current session key yields zero access to historical or future conversation transcripts.

3. Ephemeral Local Shredding

Message decay is local and definitive. Once the burn timer elapses, cryptographic keys in volatile memory are overwritten with random noise.

4. Hardware Enclave Storage

Identity keys never leave the device's hardware security module (Secure Enclave on iOS/macOS, StrongBox Keymaster on Android).

Team Vision

Vespera is engineered by an independent security and cryptography collective dedicated to sovereign tools. We believe human autonomy relies on unmonitored speech, verifiable mathematics, and open standards.
