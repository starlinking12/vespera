ABOUT / VESPERA

Privacy should feel natural.

Vespera is an exploration of what a quieter messaging experience could look like. Clear choices, considered motion, and space to think.

01 / THE IDEA

Conversation before clutter.

A messaging interface should give your words space. Vespera's visual concept keeps controls close when you need them and out of the way when you don't.

02 / IDENTITY

A handle to begin.

The proposed identity model starts with a handle rather than putting a phone number at the centre of every conversation. The final implementation and its privacy properties remain to be tested.

03 / CONTROL

Decide what remains.

The interactive preview demonstrates local and everyone-delete choices alongside Silent Lifespan options of Off, 1h, 24h, and 7d. It does not connect to another device or remove real messages.

04 / TRUST

Proof belongs before promises.

Encryption, key verification, deletion guarantees, and platform support require implementation and independent review. The design shows an intent, not a verified security claim.
